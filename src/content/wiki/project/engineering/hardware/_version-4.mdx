**C1.4 — V3 and the first biological run.** V3 is the design intended for replication across
the array, and the version the reproducibility goal is written against: a non-hardware team
member should be able to build one from the wiki instructions, bill of materials and
firmware.

**V3 remains the 40 mL instrument**, not the lower-volume screening tier from C1.3 — that
tier is the thesis project Radisic is advising, and nothing in it has been built. What V3
changes relative to V2 is a list of V2's own failures:

| Change | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **New fan, controllable down to 150 RPM**, with magnets added to the hub | V2 could not drive the stir bar slowly enough, and the magnet fix is what finally produced a vortex |
| **Sturdy base** | Fan and table vibration were corrupting the OD reading |
| **Status tab** showing every connected port and reactor | V1's defect — one reactor failing silenced the rest — plus a bug sending controls to *all* reactors instead of the selected one |
| **Snap-fit pump lid that actually snaps** | The V2 lid's flaps did not engage, which is why its two pump experiments never ran |
| **Three-pump architecture**, two in and one out | The requirement from C1.1, now wired, with one pump prototype fully functioning |
| **OD holder lowered, keyhole cap slot** | Closing the light gap that shifted readings when the vial was reseated |

Every row traces to a measured failure in an earlier cycle, which is the clearest evidence
the loop was actually running rather than being described after the fact.

<EngineeringStage id="test">

**The first biological run happened, and the reactor grew bacteria.**

A 20 mL *E. coli* culture ran for 4 hours in the reactor. Measured by spectrophotometry on a
0.5-diluted aliquot, **OD600 went from 0.153 to 1.25 and 1.166**. Temperature reached
37 °C ±0.1 °C in under 20 minutes and held to ±0.3 °C.

  caption="Temperature and OD600 signals across an earlier 1.5-hour run."
/>

**Two honest negatives sit beside the growth result.** The reactor's own OD600 readings
began at zero and were erratic, and a five-point ground-truth comparison against the
spectrophotometer showed **the linear relationship did not give an adequate OD curve**.

<Figure
  src="https://static.igem.wiki/teams/6187/wiki/hardware-notebook/overview/finalresult/9.avif"
  alt="Five ground-truth spectrophotometer readings against bioreactor OD readings"
  caption="Five ground-truth spectrophotometer readings against bioreactor OD. The linear relationship did not produce an adequate curve — the sensor needs fixing and calibrating across at least five concentrations before experimentation begins."
/>

Mixing also needed a magnet fix: small stir bars above 2,000 RPM failed to produce a vortex,
and only medium stir bars at slower speeds mixed adequately.

**No PETase-activity measurement was made.** The enzymatic half of the run is untested,
which is where Wet Lab's non-functional display plasmid (A1.2) bites directly — the run
needs a PETase-expressing strain, and through early October there wasn't one. The tracks are
genuinely coupled, and this is where it shows.

<EngineeringStage id="learn">

**Measured against the requirements table from C1.1**, these rows now close:

| Requirement | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Cost | **≈$204 CAD per unit**, against the Pioreactor's $441 |
| Temperature stability | ±0.3 °C, peak ~60 °C (62.3 °C at 10 mL) |
| OD | Three-point calibration, R² = 0.993 |
| Stirring | 200–6,500 RPM |
| Pumping | 6.6–23 mL/min |
| Power | ~12 W per unit; **7 reactors per 200 W charger** |
| Biology | OD600 0.153 → ~1.2 in 4 hours |

The cost figure is the Best Hardware argument in one line: **roughly half the price of the
commercial reference we benchmarked against**, built and documented by a student team.

**The rows that remain open are the thermal gap and OD accuracy in the integrated system.**
Per-reactor heating peaks near 60 °C against the 60–70 °C a degradation experiment needs,
and the OD sensor tracks OD600 in calibration but not reliably in a live run. Both are
specific, measurable, and named in the next-steps section rather than glossed.
