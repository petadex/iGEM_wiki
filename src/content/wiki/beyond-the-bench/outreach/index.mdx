## Impact

What outreach has already changed for the team and our audiences:

- **Brand clarity** — A personal, hand-drawn visual language (earthy tones, enzyme characters, no AI art) now guides wiki and social work.
- **Audience reach** — Project Intro post and the first newsletter put season storytelling in front of the mailing list (300+ contacts) and Instagram followers.
- **Subteam storytelling** — Road to Jamboree hardware reels and meeting coverage help other teams explain their work publicly.
- **Conference legitimacy** — Confirmed seminar and panel speakers for Sep 19 give the microplastics event a concrete public program.
- **Promo production** — At Meet #15 (~Jul 20), ~15 of 35 live shots were in and editing had started (CapCut + DaVinci); TED-Ed-style animated mid-section (shots 2.1–2.9 + 3.12) runs in parallel with an Aug 3 internal animation deadline.
- **Presentation track** — A separate 15-minute presentation-video crew is forming so promo bandwidth isn’t overloaded; HP stakeholder interviews are the next content gap.
