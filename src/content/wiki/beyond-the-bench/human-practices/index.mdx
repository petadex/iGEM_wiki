## Events

Public and team moments where HP work showed up this season.

### May 9 — Team social (with Trash Team)

Joint social announced in early HP meetings; outreach covered photo/video for Instagram and the wiki.

### June 15–17 — Synbio poster (University of Waterloo)

Poster presentation accepted mid-season. HP used the window to practice project framing for an academic audience while stakeholder outreach continued.

### July 12 — Bioethics Symposium (virtual)

Sunday symposium, 2–4pm EST. Each team gave a 10–15 minute ethics presentation plus Q&A, guided by beneficence, non-maleficence, justice, and autonomy — plus equitable access and scale-up design questions.

### July 23 — Meet 15: dual feedback track

After the symposium, HP split stakeholder meetings into (1) PETadex website feedback and (2) feedback on the regulatory / socioeconomic / sustainability reports. Follow-ups to prior PETase science contacts began here.

### September 19 — Microplastics conference

Save-the-date shared with [Outreach](/beyond-the-bench/outreach/#events). Seminar and panel speakers (including Miriam Diamond and Karen Wirsig) support Integrated HP storytelling for the wiki and Jamboree.
