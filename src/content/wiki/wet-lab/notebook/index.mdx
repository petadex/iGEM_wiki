## Overview

This section outlines the protocols and experiments that were used for general preparatory work for the assays. This includes the assembly of the AS and PD plasmids and transformation into the expression strain.

## Assembling the AS Plasmid

The full AS plasmid contains the AS empty backbone (AS-E), the copper-inducible promoter, pCusC, and an assay specific insert that is either msGFP2 for the Biosensor assay or sul1/sul2 for the Selection assay. The full constructs are referred to respectively as AS-msGFP2 or AS-sul1/sul2. To engineer these constructs, AS-E was commercially synthesized as a linear fragment, after which a KLD reaction was used to circularize the plasmid. AS-E contains BsaI recognition sites, allowing for the insertion of the pCusC and msGFP2 or sul1/sul2 via GGA. Figure 1 illustrates the workflow for assembling the AS plasmid.

The KLD reaction was performed using the NEB KLD Enzyme Mix and protocol. The reaction mix was prepared using the amounts shown in Table 1, incubated at room temperature for 5 minutes, and stored at -20℃.

Table 1. 10 uL KLD reaction used to circularize AS-E.

| **Reagent**              | **Volume** |
| ------------------------ | ---------- |
| Linear AS fragment       | 1 µL       |
| KLD Reaction Buffer (2X) | 5 µL       |
| KLD Enzyme Mix (10X)     | 1 µL       |
| Nuclease-free Water      | 3 µL       |
| **Total Volume**         | **10 µL**  |

CaCl2 DH5a competent cells made in-house were used to replicate the AS-E KLD product, and the Monarch® Spin Plasmid Miniprep Kit was used with our protocol optimizations to isolate the plasmid.

The NEBridge® Golden Gate Assembly Kit (BsaI-HF® v2) kit was used to insert the msGFP2 and sul1/sul2 fragments to produce the full AS plasmids. The GGA reaction mix was prepared by gently mixing the amounts specified in Table 2.

Table 2. 20 uL GGA reaction used for assembly of full AS plasmid.

| **Reagent**                             | **Amount**                     |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| AS-E miniprepped (75 ng/µL)             | 1 µL                           |
| msGFP2 or sul1/sul2 insert              | 2 insert: 1 vector molar ratio |
| T4 DNA Ligase Buffer (10X)              | 2 µL                           |
| NEB Golden Gate Enzyme Mix (BsaI-HF-v2) | 1 µL                           |
| Nuclease-free Water                     | X µL                           |
| **Total Volume**                        | **20 µL**                      |

Then, the reaction was incubated in a thermocycler for 5 minutes at 37 ℃. Two GGA reactions were performed: one for AS-msGFP2 and one for AS-sul1/sul2. The GGA product was transformed into CaCl2 DH5a competent cells, grown, and miniprepped.

## The PETase-display Plasmid and Library Construction

PD-V2 plasmids containing control PETase inserts with known activity, including IsPETase, Fast-PETase, LCC, M4-Q PETase, and Dead_IsPETase, were commercially synthesized to expedite the testing process before working with the PETase library generated by Dry Lab’s algorithm. The PD-V2 empty backbone (PD-E) was also commercially synthesized as a circular plasmid with BsmBI recognition sites to allow for library construction. GGA was performed using the same protocol shown in Table 2 to construct the PETase library, substituting for the PD plasmid fragments and the NEBridge® Golden Gate Enzyme Mix (BsmBI-HF® v2).

## Serial Transformation into E. coli BL21(DE3)

BL21(DE3) transformed with AS plasmid was made chemically competent using CaCl2 to prepare for serial transformation. Following this, PD plasmid was transformed into the AS competent cells and used for assay testing (Figure 2). Serial transformation was chosen to reduce library loss that would occur with cotransformation.
