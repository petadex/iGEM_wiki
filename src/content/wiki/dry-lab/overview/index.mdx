## PETamon Game

Gotta degrade 'em all! You start with one enzyme, IsPETase, and a beach covered in plastic film. Each battle you win lets you sequence the enzyme the next site needs, and your growing team heads into hotter, harsher sites to battle hardier plastic types. The game takes you on an odyssey through the environments where plastic is found: polyurethane foam in a landfill, fishing line at a nylon works only an amidase can cut, a sorting line, a 72°C recycling reactor, and a final boss plastic that no known enzyme can break down. No single enzyme can clean up every plastic type on Earth, so we built PETadex to find the best enzyme for each and every application.

*Stats are made up for play, but the problem and the story are very real.*
